# Which sync mode matches the wall best, and does Vivid beat the others?

- **Date:** 2026-11-16
- **Branch:** `exp/best-mode`
- **Build id:** 2.0.0-dev line, hub on commit `193d10f` (the build id was not yet in `/api/status`)
- **Hardware (reported):** NodeMCU ESP8266 + WLED 16.0.1, 180 monitor LEDs + 201 bed LEDs on TX, one 5 V 5 A adapter. Lenovo FHD Webcam, picked by name. Colour matrix last calibrated 2026-11-04.

## Hypothesis and pass mark

Vivid (Enriched's tone stage plus part of Cinema's colour flood) gives the best match of the four modes. Pass: lowest mean colour mismatch over the colourful scenes, and no scene worse than Cinema by more than 0.1.

## Method

`python3 tools/mode_trial.py --camera Lenovo --regions recordings/calibration-regions.npz --mode movie --mode game --mode enriched --mode vivid --mode "vivid-lit=vivid:immersion=0.45" --mode "vivid-deep=vivid:immersion=0.15" --passes 2`

Eight scenes shown full screen, hub brightness 100% during the run (restored after), two passes with the mode order reversed on the second. Same scenes, same camera position for every mode.

## Results

Colour mismatch, 0 = the wall matches the screen (lower is better):

| scene | Cinema | Gaming | Enriched | Vivid | Vivid-lit | Vivid-deep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| skin | 0.109 | 0.123 | 0.159 | 0.144 | 0.168 | 0.181 |
| white_page | 0.160 | 0.141 | 0.172 | 0.172 | 0.184 | 0.178 |
| game_colour | 0.126 | 0.164 | 0.195 | 0.066 | 0.024 | 0.112 |
| bright_day | 0.485 | 0.414 | 0.289 | 0.588 | 0.582 | 0.560 |
| mid_grade (warm) | 0.939 | 1.006 | 0.807 | 0.897 | 0.973 | 1.088 |

Latency, Vivid (blend 0.5), 20 flashes: glass-to-LED median 70.4 ms, p95 84.6 ms; screen-to-send on the Mac p50 29.8 ms.

Not usable: the camera's wall-to-screen "glow" ratio (auto-exposure blanked bright screens and only the noise floor showed in dark ones), and mismatch on black and the two dark scenes (no colour to compare). The tool no longer reports them. The software figures for brightness and darkness (computed LED levels) stand on their own.

## Decision

**Dropped: the webcam cannot judge colour on this setup.** Hypothesis not supported, and the method itself failed.

- Trial 1 (above): Vivid worst on `bright_day`; warm mid-tones bad in every mode.
- A recalibration (after two calibration fixes, merged) cut the camera's mismatch 0.40 -> 0.10, and a second trial ranked Vivid with no boost first (0.23 mean). But the fitted matrix turned blue into sky blue and magenta into salmon on the actual LED output: the webcam's own channel mixing had been "corrected" into the strip. The owner saw washed-out colour. The previous matrix was restored, then replaced by the datasheet model.
- The camera stays useful for timing: glass-to-LED median 70.4 ms, p95 84.6 ms.

What replaced it: direct checks on the hub's own output bytes (colour bars through the live pipeline) and replays of recorded per-LED colour, plus the owner's eye. Those led to the Adaptive brightness fix, hue balance, adaptive pace and Vivid as the default (see CHANGELOG).
